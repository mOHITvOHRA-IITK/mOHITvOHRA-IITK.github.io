About Me

I am an Assistant Principal Researcher in the 4IR Robotics Department at Dubai Electricity and Water Authority (DEWA) – R&D. I build and test robots that work outdoors, on real sites, for infrastructure inspection and underground utility mapping.

My work covers the full robot stack: RTK-GNSS localization, fusion of LiDAR, RGB-D and ground penetrating radar (GPR) data, 3D perception, and ROS-based autonomy. The aim is simple: systems that keep working in large, unstructured environments, not only in the lab.

I received my PhD from IIT Kanpur. My thesis, "Smart Manipulation Strategies for Autonomous Robotic Systems", is available here.

Research Areas

  • Autonomous field robotics
  • RTK-GNSS localization and multi-sensor fusion
  • SLAM and navigation outdoors and where GNSS is weak or blocked
  • 3D perception with LiDAR, RGB-D cameras and point clouds
  • Ground penetrating radar (GPR) signal processing and subsurface mapping
  • 6D pose estimation and robotic manipulation
  • ROS system integration and field deployment
